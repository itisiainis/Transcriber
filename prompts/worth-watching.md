Tell me whether this video is worth my time.

Answer in this order:

1. What is actually in it — the substance, in three or four sentences.
2. What is new here versus what any informed person already knows.
3. What is weak: oversimplified, unsupported, or there to sell something.
4. Verdict: watch it, skim, or skip it.

If the verdict is skim, list the stretches worth watching: timestamp range,
its length, and one or two sentences on what is in it and why it is worth the
time. A bare timestamp is not an answer — it just hands the decision back to
me. Finish with the total worth watching against the full length, and what the
remainder consists of.

Be blunt. Most videos are padding around a small amount of content, and if
that is the case here, say so. If the whole thing is a lead-in to a paid
product or a channel promotion, that is the most important thing to tell me.
